Questions families ask us

Short, honest answers. If yours isn’t here, a support specialist is happy to talk it through.

Is Harbor free?

Yes. Reading the guides, joining a support group and being matched with a peer mentor are all free. You don’t need an account to read anything on the site.

Who can join a Harbor support group?

Anyone whose family is touched by cancer: people living with cancer, partners and spouses, parents of children with cancer, adult children caring for a parent, kids and teens, and people who are grieving.

How do I tell my children that someone in the family has cancer?

Use simple, honest words that fit their age, say the word “cancer”, and tell them it is not their fault and they can’t catch it. Let them know who will look after them and that they can ask questions any time. Our guide on talking with children has age-by-age wording.

Does Harbor give medical advice?

No. Harbor offers emotional and practical support. Questions about diagnosis, treatment or medicines should always go to your care team.

What should I do if I’m in crisis?

In the US, call or text 988 any time to reach the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.
