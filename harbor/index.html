You don’t have to carry this alone.

Cancer Family Support helps patients, partners, parents, kids and caregivers find steady ground through cancer, with plain-language guides, practical help, and people who have been where you are.

    Find your people

    Small, gentle groups led by trained facilitators, and one-on-one time with someone who understands. All free. Join, listen, or just sit in.

    What a first group is like

    • You can keep your camera off, or just listen.
    • Groups are small and led by a trained facilitator.
    • What’s shared in the circle stays in the circle.
    • Leave whenever you need to. No explanation needed.

    Save a seat

    Pick a group you’d like to join. Online sign-up is opening soon.

    Need a group today? CancerCare and the Cancer Support Community (1-888-793-9355) both run free groups, online and by phone.
